**Uncharted: The Nathan Drake Collection** PC port by **BilalReloaded**
([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@BilalReloaded/videos), [GitHub](https://github.com/bc160201983)).
Support the work on [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/c/bilalreloaded).

On 0.4.1 or older? The launcher offers this update: press **UPDATE TO 0.5.0** next to PLAY.

A big update: the most common crashes fixed, smoother play on laptops, and new features:
trophies, 5.1/7.1 surround sound, the controller light bar and every reward unlockable.

## New

- **Trophies**: earned trophies are saved and shown with a PS5-style popup (the trophy's picture
  from your copy of the game and a short chime). The in-game menu (Insert) has a Trophies tab
  with every trophy of the running game. Trophy names are encrypted in the game files and are not
  shown. Trophies earned with earlier versions were not saved, so the count starts at zero.
- **5.1 and 7.1 surround sound**: the games' own surround mixes, picked from your Windows sound
  output (launcher Advanced -> Speakers: Auto, Stereo, 5.1 or 7.1). Stereo and headphones keep
  the games' stereo mix.
- **Unlock all rewards** (launcher Advanced): every reward in Bonuses (skins, weapons, render
  modes, art galleries, gameplay tweaks) and every story cutscene, in all three games, usable
  without finishing the game first. Nothing is saved: turned off, you see your own progress.
- **Controller light bar**: DualShock 4 and DualSense show the games' light bar colours.
- **US version (CUSA02320)** supported, and **Install game from .pkg** in the launcher.
- **Mouse options**: aim sensitivity, up/down speed, invert left/right; smoother mouse aiming.
- **Sharper moving characters with DLSS / FSR** (#84, thanks to kuraeow): characters now carry
  their own motion vectors, so faces, hair and clothes no longer smear; and the screen wipe covers
  the whole screen below 720p (Steam Deck).

## Fixed

- **Drake's Fortune: the most common crash** ("execute access to 0", #27 #30 #42 #62 #94 #102
  #107), and the same crash in **Uncharted 2 chapter 5** (#109).
- **Uncharted 2: crash on a garbage texture** ("image_info.cpp UpdateSize: Assertion Failed").
- **Multithreaded rendering no longer makes objects flicker** (flat white objects in Uncharted 2,
  flat grey weapons in Drake's Fortune, #23), at no FPS cost.
- **Multi-second freezes** in all three games from shader compiles (#71 #74 #79 #105).
- **Crash or very long start while loading shaders** for players of earlier versions (#92 #95):
  old caches kept thousands of unused shader versions; they are removed once.
- **Wrong-looking surfaces** from a damaged shader cache are repaired.
- **Memory growth** over long sessions (#95): video memory is given back, a leak of ~140 MB a
  minute is gone.
- **Stutter when the next area loads** on most laptops and integrated GPUs (Resizable BAR).
- **Shader cache kept per graphics card** on laptops that switch between integrated and dedicated
  graphics.
- **Drake's Deception: multithreaded rendering on by default** (60 FPS where one CPU thread was
  the limit on an RTX 4060 laptop).
- Aiming blur above 1080p (Uncharted 2 and 3), the Uncharted 3 d-pad keyboard prompt, a warning
  for USB/SD/exFAT drives, less "out of device memory" on small cards (#81).

Full details in [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/bc160201983/Uncharted-The-Nathan-Drake-Collection-PC-port/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

## Known issues

- **Japanese, Korean and Chinese** can't be selected in the European copy (#108). Being looked at.
- **Movies (FMV) slower than the sound at 4K** for some players (#77). Being looked at.
- **Uncharted 2 in busy areas on laptops** (Nepal streets): turn on Advanced -> Multithreaded
  rendering for 60 FPS where the CPU is the limit.

## Install (new players)

1. Download **Uncharted-windows.zip** below and extract it anywhere.
2. Start **Uncharted Launcher.exe** and select your game folder (your dump, for example
   `CUSA02344`, with `eboot.bin` and `sce_sys`). Update 1.02 is recommended: next to the game
   folder named like it plus `-patch`, chosen in Advanced -> GAME UPDATE, or copied into the
   game folder.
3. Pick a game and a preset and press **PLAY**. Graphics cards with 6 GB of video memory or more.
